Comparative Big Data Analytics of Online Food Delivery Platforms

1. KEY DATASET FINDINGS

Swiggy
139,321 records were analyzed. There were 105,864 records with numeric ratings, with an average numeric rating of approximately 4.04/5. The average extracted listed price for two was approximately 270.34 in the source's assumed INR convention.

Zomato
101,530 item records were analyzed. The average item rating was approximately 3.89/5, and the average listed item price was approximately 243.69 in the source price units.

Grubhub
8,088 restaurant records were analyzed. The average review rating was approximately 3.40/5, average estimated delivery time was 42.54 minutes, average delivery fee was 2.45, and average service fee was 6.16 in the source fee units.

Restaurant listings
The analysis-ready restaurant listing dataset contains 63,469 records. Its summary report describes score, review-count, category, address, and coordinate coverage.

Restaurant menus
5,082,180 menu records and 63,309 distinct restaurant IDs were analyzed. There were 4,907,926 positive parsed prices, 174,251 zero prices, and 3 unparsed prices.

3. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS

These findings are descriptive statistics from separate datasets. They do not establish that one delivery platform is better than another. The sources differ in geography, collection period, observation unit, rating definitions, and price conventions. Currency and distance units should be verified before comparisons. The datasets are snapshots, so delivery estimates and fees should not be treated as current values.

4. CONCLUSION

The project demonstrates a workflow for auditing, cleaning, validating, and exploring large heterogeneous datasets. It combines distributed processing where appropriate with streaming approaches for large CSV files. The analysis produces descriptive insights while documenting data quality issues and comparison limitations. A fair platform comparison requires harmonized definitions, units, currency, and geographical coverage.
